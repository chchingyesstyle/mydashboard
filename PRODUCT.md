# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Commuters travelling between Watford Junction and London Euston in either direction. They usually glance at the dashboard on an always-on household tablet or monitor while preparing to leave, but may also check it on a phone. A future reTerminal E1001 ePaper client will serve the same at-a-glance use case.

## Product Purpose

Provide one trustworthy view of every direct departure in the selected Watford Junction–London Euston direction, including London Overground, alongside weather at the departure station. Success means a commuter can understand the next useful trains, platforms, disruption state, and local weather within seconds.

## Brand Personality

Calm, immediate, trustworthy. The voice is concise and factual without sounding mechanical. Live information should feel dependable, while stale or unavailable information should be disclosed plainly.

## Anti-references

This is not a ticket retailer, journey planner, generic admin dashboard, or configurable widget marketplace. It should not use SaaS-style metric cards, decorative glass effects, neon transport styling, or copy HKDashboard branding and assets. It should not bury the route behind navigation or settings.

## Design Principles

1. Glance first: prioritize departure time, expected status, and platform in that order.
2. Stay route-focused: every visible feature must support the selected Watford Junction–Euston direction.
3. Tell the truth about freshness: stale, partial, and unavailable data must be immediately understandable.
4. Keep every direct option: do not hide London Overground or cancelled services.
5. Share one contract: browser and future ePaper clients consume the same stable meaning.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG AA contrast. Communicate status with text and icons as well as color. Support keyboard navigation, visible focus, reduced motion, touch-friendly controls, responsive layouts, and readable information density on both phone and landscape displays.
